If one person tells you to hike the Dish, ten more will. They're right.

## The basics

- **Distance:** about 3.5 miles as a loop, with a couple of steep climbs.
- **Hours:** dawn to dusk — the gates close, so plan around sunset.
- **Rules:** no bikes, no dogs, stay on the paved trail.

## Getting there

The easiest entrance from campus is the Stanford Avenue gate. From the Main Quad it's about a 20-minute walk or a quick bike ride — the [map](/map) has it pinned under **Rec & sports**.

## Why it's worth it

From the top you can see the whole Bay, the campus laid out below, and on clear days all the way to San Francisco. Bring water and go about an hour before sunset.
