Finding a good study spot is half the battle. These are the ones I've tested so far.

## 1. Bender Room, Green Library

Tall windows, long tables and dead silence. My go-to for reading.

## 2. Huang basement

Group study rooms you can book, plus whiteboards. Ideal for problem-set crunch time.

## 3. Lathrop Library

Open late, with printing and a big tech lounge. Right next to Meyer Green.

## 4. Coupa Café at Y2E2

Not quiet, but great for working with friends and caffeine within arm's reach.

## 5. Outside on the Oval

When the weather's good (so, most of the year), a blanket on the grass beats any desk.

Every spot here is pinned on the [campus map](/map) under **Libraries** and **Food & coffee**.
