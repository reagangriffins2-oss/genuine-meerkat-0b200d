Campus is roughly two miles across, and classes are usually ten minutes apart. That's tight if you're walking from the Main Quad to Gates. Here's how I plan my days.

## Rough travel times

| From → To | Walk | Bike |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Quad → Gates CS | 7 min | 3 min |
| Main Quad → Tresidder | 5 min | 2 min |
| FloMo → Huang | 12 min | 4 min |
| Main Quad → Caltrain | 25 min | 8 min |

The [map](/map) can estimate these for you — hit **Locate me**, pick a place, and it shows walking and biking times.

## The Marguerite shuttle

It's free, it's open to everyone, and the main hub is by the bookstore on White Plaza. The lines I use most go to the Palo Alto Caltrain station and the Shopping Center.

## Landmarks that save me

1. **Hoover Tower** — visible from almost everywhere. Tower on your right while facing MemChu? You're in the Quad.
2. **The Oval** — head here and you'll find Palm Drive and the way off campus.
3. **White Plaza** — the crossroads. Bookstore, Tresidder and the Claw fountain.
