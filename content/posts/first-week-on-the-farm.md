Everyone warned me Stanford was big. Nobody warned me that every building on the Main Quad looks *exactly the same* for the first three days.

## The Quad, decoded

The trick that finally clicked for me: the Main Quad buildings are numbered, and the numbers roughly go around the arcade. Building **01** sits by the front arch facing the Oval, and **200** — Lane History Corner — is on the corner closest to Hoover Tower. Once you know which side Memorial Church is on (the back), you can orient yourself from anywhere inside.

I've pinned the Quad, MemChu and Hoover Tower on the [campus map](/map) so you can see how they line up.

## Bikes, bikes, everywhere

The roundabouts near the Engineering Quad are no joke at ten minutes to the hour. Rules I've picked up:

- Yield to whoever is already in the circle.
- Signal with your hand, not with your voice.
- Lock your bike to a rack — not a tree or a railing — or it might get impounded.

## Why "the Farm"?

The campus was built on Leland Stanford's old horse farm, and the nickname stuck. Some days, cycling past the foothills at sunset, it still feels like one.
